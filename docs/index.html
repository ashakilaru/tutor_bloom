Tutor Bloom Support

Tuition fees, made simple. Tutor Bloom helps independent tutors organize students, monthly fees and payment records.

Contact support

Email kilarupinky@gmail.com for help, bug reports or privacy questions. Include your app version and describe the issue. Please do not send student names, phone numbers or payment records.

Getting started

Add a student, set their monthly fee and joining month, then record payments you have already received. Choose a month to view its collected and outstanding fees. Months before a student joins do not create a fee balance.

Keep a backup

Version 1.0.0 stores records on your device. Use the app’s backup option and keep the copy somewhere private. Uninstalling the app or losing the device may erase records. We cannot recover records that have not been backed up.

Reminders and payments

Copy a reminder or open it in WhatsApp and review it before sending. Tutor Bloom tracks payments; it does not collect or transfer money.
