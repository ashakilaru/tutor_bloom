Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026. This policy covers Tutor Bloom for iOS, version 1.0.0 (build 3), operated by Asha Kilaru.

Records stay on your device

Tutor Bloom stores the student names, subjects, phone numbers, joining months, fees and payment records you enter locally on your device. This version does not require an account or upload those records to a Tutor Bloom server. It does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Sharing and backups you choose

When you copy a reminder or backup, its contents are placed on your device clipboard. When you choose WhatsApp, the recipient phone number and prepared message are passed to WhatsApp. Review recipients and contents before sending. WhatsApp and any service you choose to store a backup operate under their own privacy policies. Your device’s operating-system backup settings may also affect how local app data is backed up.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond and troubleshoot, retain it only as needed for support and applicable obligations, and do not sell it or use it for advertising. Email is handled through our email provider. Avoid including identifiable student records.

Managing your information

You control the records and backup copies you create. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; copies in backups, messages or other services must be managed separately. Contact us to request deletion of information you sent to support.

Student information

The app is intended for tutors to manage their own records. It does not offer student accounts. Tutors are responsible for entering and sharing student information appropriately and obtaining any necessary permission.

This support website

These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or contact forms. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the website.

Updates and contact

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. For support or privacy questions, contact kilarupinky@gmail.com.
